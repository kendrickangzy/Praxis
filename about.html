Who We Are

An education centre designed by IB students, for IB students.

Our Vision

PRAXIS is a tutoring centre that specialises in the Humanities subjects, EE, TOK and IA for the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IBDP).

We started to solve real problems. Packed classrooms with little to no private attention, insufficient personalised feedback, sparse notes tailored to each school’s curriculum.

That’s why we have:

  • Classes of at most 8 students.
  • A compulsory 4-day marking turnover system.
  • A team of experienced tutors making comprehensive notes tailored to every single student.

This is the PRAXIS difference.

Meet Our Team

Our Tutors

All our tutors are graduates of the IBDP, have attained exemplary grades (43 and above; A for the EE) and are undergraduates of top universities.

What this means for YOU: you’re taught by someone who’s cracked the code. You’re taught to succeed in the system.

Our methodologies are tailored to scoring in the IB: rooted in the marking criteria, comprehensive notes for a one-stop solution, and curricula that follow each school’s timetable.
