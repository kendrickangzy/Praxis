Who We Are

Our Vision

PRAXIS Education is a Singapore-based tuition centre that specialises in English A: Language and Literature (LL), Economics, Extended Essay (EE), Theory of Knowledge (TOK) and Internal Assessment (IA) for the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IBDP).

We started PRAXIS Education to solve real problems: packed classrooms with little to no private attention, insufficient personalised feedback and sparse notes tailored to each school’s curriculum.
