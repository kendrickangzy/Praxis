About Praxis

Tutoring built around
the way IB actually works

At Praxis, we don't just teach content — we teach the IB system. Language and Literature is as much about assessment strategy as it is about literary analysis, and our tutors know every mark scheme inside out.

Whether you're struggling with unseen commentary, trying to structure a comparative essay, or preparing for your Individual Oral, we tailor every session to where you are right now and where you need to be.

  • 100% IB-focused — we only teach what's on your syllabus
  • Flexible online sessions that fit around your school timetable
  • Detailed written feedback on every essay and practice paper

What We Offer

Paper 1 — Guided Literary Analysis

Master the unseen commentary. We drill the analytical framework, teach you how to read any text under time pressure, and give you a structure that scores consistently.

Paper 2 — Comparative Essay

Build essays that compare across texts with clarity and precision. We focus on thesis construction, point-evidence-analysis, and meeting all four assessment criteria.

  • ✓ Essay planning techniques

Individual Oral (IO) Prep

The IO is 15 minutes that can make or break your grade. We run full mock orals, help you choose the right extract, and coach your delivery from first draft to exam day.

  • ✓ Extract selection strategy
  • ✓ Full mock oral + recording review
  • ✓ Global issue framing
