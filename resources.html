Common questions about the course

Paper 1 is an unseen literary commentary — you're given a previously unseen text and must analyse it in a timed essay. Paper 2 is a comparative essay based on texts you've studied during the course, responding to an unseen question.
The IO is 15 minutes in total — 10 minutes for your presentation and analysis of a chosen extract, followed by 5 minutes of questions from your teacher.
A global issue is a significant topic relevant to the real world and to both the literary and non-literary texts you discuss. It must be specific enough to allow real analysis — for example, "the construction of female identity in post-colonial societies" rather than just "identity."
