Open Roles

Where could you fit in?

Teaching

IB Tutor

Teach IB Humanities subjects to small classes of up to 8 students. You'll build analytical frameworks, give detailed written feedback, and guide students from where they are to where they want to be.

  • IB Diploma score of 43 or above
  • Grade 6 or 7 in the subject you'd teach
  • Articulate, patient, and genuinely invested in students

Growth

Marketing

Drive awareness and enrolment through social media, content, and partnerships. You'll own the PRAXIS brand voice and grow our presence across Instagram, TikTok, and beyond.

  • Strong eye for design and copywriting
  • Comfortable with Instagram, TikTok, and short-form content
  • Data-driven and highly organised

Technology

Software Developer

Build and maintain the tools that power PRAXIS — our website, internal dashboards, and student-facing platforms. You'll have real ownership over a production codebase from day one.

  • Proficient in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
  • Experience with a modern framework a plus
  • Cares about clean, maintainable code

Operations

Finance & Operations

Keep PRAXIS running smoothly. From billing and scheduling to financial reporting and vendor management — you'll be the backbone that lets the rest of the team focus on what they do best.

  • Highly organised and detail-oriented
  • Comfortable with financial spreadsheets and reporting
  • Proactive and solutions-oriented

Content

Content Creator

Create the educational content that puts PRAXIS on the map — study guides, revision videos, and social media posts that genuinely help IB students. If you can make complex literary analysis feel accessible, we want to hear from you.

  • Excellent written English
  • IB background a strong plus
  • Comfortable on camera or behind the keyboard
  • Creative, self-directed, and consistent
