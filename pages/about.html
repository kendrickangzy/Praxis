Our Story

Tutoring built from inside the IB system

Praxis was founded by a tutor who went through the IB themselves and later worked as a teaching assistant at an IB school. That inside experience means we don't just teach content — we teach the system. We know exactly what examiners are looking for and how to help students meet those criteria consistently.

The name "Praxis" comes from the Greek for practice and application — because that's exactly what separates good students from great ones. Theory alone doesn't get you a 7. Deliberate, structured practice does.

  • First-hand experience with the IB Diploma Programme
  • Deep knowledge of mark schemes and assessment criteria
  • Over 100 students tutored across 20+ IB schools worldwide
